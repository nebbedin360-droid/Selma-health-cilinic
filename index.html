Telemedicine

See a doctor without leaving home

Perfect for follow-ups, prescriptions and second opinions — or when you're in Dire Dawa, Hawassa or anywhere else in Ethiopia. All you need is a phone with a camera.

    Book a video slot

    Choose Telemedicine on the booking form and pick a time. You'll get a confirmation call with your doctor's name.

    Get your secure link

    We send a private video link by SMS and Telegram. It opens in your browser — no app to download.

Questions

Everything you might be wondering

Can't find your answer? Call us on +251 911 234 567 — a real person answers, day or night.

Prefer to talk it through?

Reception answers 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

Walk-ins are welcome for general medicine and the laboratory between 8:00 AM and 6:00 PM. For specialist departments like dental, eye care and pediatrics we strongly recommend booking — it means no waiting and you get the doctor you asked for.

How much does a consultation cost?

A standard general consultation is 500 ETB and a specialist consultation is 800 ETB. Laboratory and imaging are priced separately and quoted before we run anything, so there are no surprises. We accept Telebirr, CBE Birr, M-PESA, Amole, cash and card.

Do you accept insurance?

Yes. We work with Nyala Insurance, Awash Insurance, United Insurance and several employer health schemes. Bring your membership card and we'll handle the paperwork at reception. If you're unsure whether your provider covers us, call us and we'll check before you come in.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Your ID, any previous medical records or test results, and a list of medications you're currently taking (including anything traditional or over-the-counter). If you're bringing a child, their vaccination card is very helpful.

Is there parking at the Bole clinic?

Yes — free patient parking is available on site, with an attendant from 7:30 AM. The entrance on Africa Avenue is step-free, and we have a wheelchair available at reception if you need one.

Can I get my test results without coming back in?

Most laboratory results are ready the same day. Our reception team will send routine results to you by Telegram or email, and your doctor calls you directly if anything needs a follow-up visit. Complex imaging results are always explained in person.

Do you offer services in languages other than Amharic?

Yes. Our clinical and reception staff speak Amharic, English, Oromo and Tigrinya. If you'd prefer a specific language, mention it on the booking form and we'll match you with the right team member.

Is there an emergency service at night?

Our 24/7 emergency line on +251 911 234 567 is answered around the clock. The Bole clinic has an on-call doctor during opening hours, and after hours the line will direct you to the nearest facility equipped for your situation.
